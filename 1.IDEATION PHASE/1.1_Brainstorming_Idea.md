# Document 1.1 — Brainstorming Idea

**Project:** Hospital Appointment Management System
**Platform:** ServiceNow (Scoped Application)
**Phase:** Ideation

---

## 1. Problem Area Identification

Healthcare appointment scheduling is one of the most persistent operational pain points in small and mid-sized clinics and hospital outpatient departments. Most facilities still rely on manual registers, phone calls, or fragmented spreadsheet-based systems to manage patient bookings with doctors. This creates several recurring issues:

- Double-booking of the same doctor time slot by different front-desk staff or over the phone
- No real-time visibility into which doctors are available on a given day or time
- Cancelled appointment slots going unused because there is no automated way to notify waitlisted patients
- Inconsistent record-keeping across registers, making it difficult to track a patient's appointment history
- No structured way to distinguish between a patient who checked in, completed a consultation, or simply never showed up

This problem area was selected because it directly maps to a workflow-automation and data-management use case — a strong fit for a ServiceNow scoped application, which is built around structured tables, role-based access, and process automation.

---

## 2. Raw Brainstormed Ideas

The following ideas were considered during initial ideation. Some were carried into the final build; others were explicitly scoped out to keep the project focused and achievable within the capstone timeline.

1. **Core appointment booking system** (patient books a slot with a doctor based on real-time availability) — *selected, built*
2. **Doctor availability and slot management module** (doctors define their own working slots) — *selected, built*
3. **Automated waitlist promotion** (when a booked slot is cancelled, the next waitlisted patient is automatically promoted) — *selected, built*
4. **Front-desk/admin assisted booking** for walk-in patients or patients without portal access — *selected, built*
5. **SMS/email appointment reminders** integrated with a third-party messaging gateway — *considered but out of scope* (requires external integration/credentials beyond the capstone's platform-only scope)
6. **Telemedicine/video consultation integration** — *considered but out of scope* (adds a media/streaming dependency unrelated to the core scheduling problem)
7. **Insurance verification and claims pre-check** — *considered but out of scope* (a separate domain problem requiring external insurance APIs)
8. **Prescription and e-EHR (electronic health record) module** — *considered but out of scope* (a much larger clinical-records problem, not an appointment-scheduling one)

---

## 3. Selected Core Idea and Justification

The selected core idea is a **ServiceNow scoped application for hospital appointment management**, centered on real-time slot booking, doctor-managed availability, and automated waitlist promotion, delivered through a custom Service Portal.

This idea was chosen over the alternatives above because it isolates the actual root cause of the observed problem — the lack of a single, automated, conflict-free system for matching patient demand to doctor availability — without expanding into adjacent domains (messaging, telemedicine, insurance, clinical records) that would dilute the technical focus of the capstone. It also plays directly to ServiceNow's core strengths: structured tables (GlideRecord), business rules and workflow automation for the waitlist promotion logic, Access Control Lists (ACLs) for role separation between Patient, Doctor, and Front Desk/Admin, and a Service Portal front end for a realistic, usable interface. This made it possible to build a functionally complete, backend-verifiable system rather than a surface-level prototype.

---

## 4. Initial Feature List (Mapped to What Was Actually Built)

- Patient registration and profile management
- Doctor registration and profile management, including specialization, qualification, and consultation fee
- Doctor-managed availability slots (create, view, block slots by date/time)
- Real-time slot browsing and booking by patients
- Atomic booking transaction to prevent double-booking of the same slot
- Appointment lifecycle tracking: Booked → Checked-In → Completed / Cancelled / No-Show
- Waitlist registration for fully booked doctors/dates
- Automated waitlist promotion when a booked slot is cancelled or marked no-show
- Front Desk/Admin portal view for manual walk-in booking and oversight
- Role-based Service Portal experience with separate tabs for Patient, Doctor, and Front Desk/Admin workflows
- Backend data verification — every portal action is reflected in the underlying ServiceNow tables
